## The brief

Pablo needed to send data underwater, and commercial acoustic communication systems vary a lot in range, reliability and price. The question wasn't "which one is best on paper". It was "which one will actually work in our conditions, at a cost that makes sense".

## What I did

- **Set the yardstick.** I evaluated the systems against the real operating conditions, range and reliability requirements for the integration.
- **Wrote the test methods.** Structured methodologies to characterise signal behaviour, sensitivity to the environment and reliability across different configurations.
- **Turned results into a decision.** I pulled everything into a feasibility and cost comparison, which drove the vendor choice and the deployment plan.

## How it went

This was as much a purchasing decision as a technical one. The most useful thing I delivered wasn't a test result, it was a clear recommendation the client could act on, with the costs and trade-offs laid out.
