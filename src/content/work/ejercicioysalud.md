## The brief

The Official College of Physicians of Zaragoza needed a public platform for clinical exercise prescription: guidance on how people with cardiovascular conditions can exercise safely. It had to hold a lot of clinical material, work in more than one language and meet institutional standards. And it had to be ready in two weeks.

## What I did

- **Planned the structure first.** I set up the site architecture and content model around multilingual educational material, clinical guides and embedded video training modules, so doctors could add to it later without fighting the layout.
- **Built the front end.** Responsive and accessible, to the college's standards.
- **Handed it over properly.** I delivered it production ready and left a codebase the college can maintain on its own.

## How it went

It went from brief to production in a two week engagement. The site is live at [ejercicioysalud.org](https://ejercicioysalud.org).
